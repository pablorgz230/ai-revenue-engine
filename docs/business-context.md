## 3. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Firmographic Criteria

- B2B SaaS company
- 50–1,000 employees
- Based in or primarily operating within Europe
- Established commercial organization
- Dedicated sales and/or revenue operations functions
- Significant recurring revenue model

### Technographic Criteria

Preferred technologies include:

- HubSpot
- Salesforce
- Microsoft Dynamics
- Common marketing automation platforms
- Business intelligence / analytics platforms

### Business Signals

Positive signals include:

- Rapid employee growth
- Recent funding round
- Expansion into new European markets
- Increasing sales headcount
- Hiring for Sales Operations or Revenue Operations roles
- Increasing marketing activity
- Implementation of a CRM
- Multiple revenue-generating teams
- Growing lead volume
- Evidence of fragmented sales and marketing processes

### Negative Signals

Negative signals include:

- B2C-only business model
- Fewer than 50 employees
- Very small sales organization
- No identifiable CRM
- Significant workforce contraction
- No meaningful sales organization
- Primarily non-recurring revenue
- Geographic focus outside Europe
- Business model incompatible with RevOps tooling
